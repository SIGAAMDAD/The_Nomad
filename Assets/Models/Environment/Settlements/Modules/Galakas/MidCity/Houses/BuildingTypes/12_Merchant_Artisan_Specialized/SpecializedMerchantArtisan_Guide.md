# Specialized Merchant and Artisan BuildingTypes

This folder contains revised commerce/artisan buildings that are meant to read differently from residential buildings.

Design changes:
- Large open shop fronts, arcaded bays, workshop counters, hoist doors, display awnings, and trade signs.
- Visible interior blockout props such as forges, looms, dye vats, shelves, counters, strongboxes, workbenches, and racks.
- More varied roof geometry: sawtooth workshop roofs, clerestory market roofs, stepped merchant gables, forge vent roofs, double-ridge market roofs, dormer arcade roofs, and asymmetric gables.

Use these around Gate Market, Grand Market Spine, Workshop Lanes, Cloth Market, and the merchant arcades to prevent the city fabric from reading as pure residential repetition.
