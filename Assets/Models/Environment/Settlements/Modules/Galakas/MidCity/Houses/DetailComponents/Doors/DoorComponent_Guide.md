# Galakas MidCity Door Components

Standalone no-material CSG door blockouts. These face **-Z**, with the origin at the ground-center threshold. They are meant to be placed into lower modules, alley overlays, shopfront variants, and final MidCity assemblies.

These components intentionally use simple CSG boxes only: leaves, frames, lintels, planks, braces, and minimal hinge markers. No materials, no meshes, no external scene dependencies.
