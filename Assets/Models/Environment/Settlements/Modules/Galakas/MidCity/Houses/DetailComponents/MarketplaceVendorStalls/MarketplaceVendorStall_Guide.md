# Galakas MidCity Marketplace Vendor Stalls

These are standalone no-material CSG vendor stalls intended for the Golden Gate / Gate Market area. They are small enough to place in rows, plazas, under arcades, or along the Grand Market Spine without baking them into building assemblies.

Recommended placement:
- Use food, water, toll, map, contract, and broker stalls immediately inside the Golden Gate.
- Use artisan sample stalls beside workshop lanes and merchant frontages.
- Use carnival / Red Mirage stalls as optional spectacle or cultural-appropriation flavor near the Gate Market.
- Keep these as separate nodes during layout so the gate plaza can be reorganized without touching buildings.

Cluster assemblies are in `BuildingTypes/13_GoldenGate_Market_StallClusters/`.
