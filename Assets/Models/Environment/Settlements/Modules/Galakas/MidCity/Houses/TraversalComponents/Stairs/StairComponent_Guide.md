# Galakas MidCity Stair Components

Standalone no-material CSG stair blockouts. Most pieces face **+Z**, with the origin at the bottom-center of the stair run. They are component-only so they can be overlaid in the total MidCity assembly without being accidentally baked into building-type assemblies too early.

The stairs are intentionally simple: block steps, optional landings, and optional low curbs. No materials, no meshes, no external scene dependencies.
